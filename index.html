Payments & Reminders
Monthly rent ledger, due tracking & reminder emails
About reminder emails: this dashboard runs entirely in your browser and has no mail server, so it can't send email on its own. "Draft Reminder" opens a preview you can edit before it hands off to your default mail app (or you can copy the text) — you press send.
With a UPI ID set, every reminder email gets its own upi://pay link pre-filled with that occupant's exact amount due — tapping it on a phone opens GPay/PhonePe/Paytm ready to pay.

Excel Data Sync
Keep an offline Excel workbook in sync with this dashboard's data
How this works: everything in this dashboard lives in your browser only. Use Export to save a full Excel workbook you can archive, edit offline, or hand to your accountant. Use Import to bring a workbook back in — matching rows (by ID) are updated, new rows are added. Always keep a backup copy before importing.
Export to Excel ⬇

Downloads one .xlsx workbook with separate sheets for Occupants, Cabins, Payments and Sales Leads — the full dataset behind this dashboard.

Just need the pending-payments list? Head to Payments for a CSV export of every due/overdue invoice with due dates and payment links.

Import from Excel ⬆

Upload a workbook exported from this dashboard (or built to match its column layout). Rows are matched by their id column — existing records get updated, unmatched rows are added as new.
